I've spent my career on the infrastructure that people only think about when it breaks.

Health-benefit portals for federal employees. Wealth platforms for families with millions invested. Mainframe environments carrying thousands of daily users.

When something goes wrong in those places, it goes wrong for a lot of people at once.

I've worked RED-alert incidents with CIOs on the line, run disaster-recovery exercises against a 48-hour clock, and took part in data-center recovery testing after 9/11.

What I learned is that reliability and security are the same discipline. A system that isn't compliant isn't stable, and a system that isn't stable can't be secured.

Today I put that into practice as a compliance and data-security analyst on the zLinux Services team. I assess systems against NIST and CIS, enforce least-privilege access, run vulnerability reviews and build the evidence that audits depend on.

I also mentor the next generation of engineers, because I've been there myself.
